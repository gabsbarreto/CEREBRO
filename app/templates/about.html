ABOUT CEREBRO

CEREBRO

A workspace for extracting research data from articles and spreadsheets.

PDF extraction

Apply a prompt to full-text PDFs using local OCR or direct OpenAI PDF input. Review each job and export its results.

Structured PDF extraction

Define worksheets with questions and rules, extract tabular results from studies, and export the workbook to Excel.

Text extraction

Map columns from CSV or Excel files into prompts, run extraction for each record, and export results alongside the original data.

Projects

Keep source files, jobs, and results organized in separate project workspaces.
